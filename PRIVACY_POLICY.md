# Privacy Policy for Allergy Scheduler

**Effective Date:** May 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PanSar ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use the "Allergy Scheduler" mobile application ("the App").

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect information to provide, improve, and protect our personal organization services.

### 1.1 Personal Information
* **Account Information:** Email address and display name provided during registration.
* **Routine Information:** To function correctly, the App allows you to log your personal allergy routines, including:
  * Allergen and schedule history.
  * Routine amounts and consistency timestamps.
  * Self-reported wellness statuses and dietary notes.

### 1.2 Usage and Technical Data
* **Crash & Performance Data:** Usage of Firebase Crashlytics to monitor stability.
* **Subscription Data:** Usage of RevenueCat to verify your wellness tier status.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
* **Routine Tracking:** To provide you with a consistent summary of your personal allergy organization process.
* **Personalized Reminders:** To help you stay on your self-defined schedule.

## 3. Important Disclaimer
**Allergy Scheduler is a tracking tool and DOES NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is designed for personal organization and schedule logging. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you have read in this application.**

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers
We share your data with the following essential service providers:
* **Google Firebase:** For authentication and encrypted database storage (Firestore).
* **RevenueCat:** For processing and managing your in-app subscriptions.

We do **NOT** sell your data to third parties or advertisers.

## 5. Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures, including SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit and Firestore Security Rules to ensure that your private data is only accessible to you and authorized members of your shared patient groups.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information immediately.

## 7. Data Deletion & Rights
We retain your information as long as your account is active. You have the right to access, correct, or erase your data at any time.

* **Account Deletion:** Users can delete their account and all associated data permanently directly within the app settings. 
* **Data Requests:** You may request a copy of your data or its deletion by contacting us at psarrid@gmail.com. Requests are typically processed within 30 days.

## 8. International Compliance (GDPR & CCPA)
We respect your privacy rights regardless of your location. Our practices are designed to align with the principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
